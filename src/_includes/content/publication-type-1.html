SIAST: A Slot Imbalance-Aware Self-Training Scheme for Semi-Supervised Slot Filling
This paper proposes a novel self-training scheme SIAST for imbalanced slot filling that aims to learn unbiased margins between slot classes while mitigating potential slot confusion, and adaptively samples pseudo-labelled data to balance the slot distribution of the training set.
Jiachi Liu, Sishi Xiong, Yuehuan He, Tong Zhou, Liwen Wang, Xuefeng Li, Bo Xiao
